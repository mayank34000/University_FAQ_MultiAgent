ACAD-022

How is the Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) calculated?

CGPA is calculated as the weighted average of grade points earned in all courses, where the weight is the credit value of each course; CGPA = sum of (grade points x credits) divided by total credits earned across all completed semesters.

Keywords: CGPA, calculation, grade points, credits, weighted average

ACAD-023

How is the Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) calculated?

SGPA is calculated using the same formula as CGPA but includes only the courses taken in that particular semester; SGPA = sum of (grade points x credits) divided by total credits registered in that semester.

Keywords: SGPA, semester performance, grade points, calculation
