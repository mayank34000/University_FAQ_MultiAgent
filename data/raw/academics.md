## ACAD-001
Q: How many total credits are required to complete the B.Tech program?
A: All B.Tech programs require a minimum of 160 credits for graduation, distributed across core courses, electives, labs, projects, and internships over eight semesters.
Tags: credits, graduation, B.Tech, total credits

## ACAD-002
Q: How many credits should a student register for in a typical semester?
A: Students are expected to register for 18-22 credits per semester; a minimum of 16 credits is mandatory unless the student is in the final semester or has been granted a reduced load by the Dean of Academics.
Tags: semester credits, credit load, registration, minimum credits

## ACAD-003
Q: What is the maximum number of credits a student can register for in a single semester?
A: The maximum credit limit per semester is 26 credits, available only to students with a CGPA of 8.5 or above in the previous semester and no active backlogs.
Tags: maximum credits, credit overload, CGPA requirement

## ACAD-019

A: The university follows a 10-point grading scale: O (Outstanding) = 10, A+ = 9, A = 8, B+ = 7, B = 6, C = 5, D = 4, F (Fail) = 0; a minimum grade of D is required to pass a course.
Tags: grading scale, grade points, 10-point scale, passing grade

## ACAD-020

## ACAD-021
Q: What is the minimum passing grade for a course?
A: The minimum passing grade is D, which requires scoring at least 40 out of 100 marks overall with a minimum of 35% in the end-term examination component.

## ACAD-022
Q: How is the Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) calculated?
A: CGPA is calculated as the weighted average of grade points earned in all courses, where the weight is the credit value of each course; CGPA = sum of (grade points x credits) divided by total credits earned across all completed semesters.
Tags: CGPA, calculation, grade points, credits, weighted average

## ACAD-023
Q: How is the Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) calculated?
A: SGPA is calculated using the same formula as CGPA but includes only the courses taken in that particular semester; SGPA = sum of (grade points x credits) divided by total credits registered in that semester.
Tags: SGPA, semester performance, grade points, calculation

## ACAD-024
Q: What is the minimum attendance requirement?
A: A minimum of 75% attendance is mandatory in each course (including lectures, tutorials, and labs) to be eligible to appear in the end-term examination for that course.
Tags: attendance, minimum requirement, 75 percent, eligibility

## ACAD-025
Q: What happens if a student's attendance falls below 75% in a course?

Tags: attendance shortage, debarment, re-registration, examination

Tags: medical leave, attendance relaxation, medical certificate, Dean of Student Affairs
