# Campus & Hostel FAQ Knowledge Base

## HOSTEL-001
Q: What are the hostel fees for AC and Non-AC rooms?
A: The hostel fees for 2026 are:
- Non-AC Room Fee: ₹80,000 per year (was ₹76,000 in 2025).
- AC Room Fee: ₹1,04,000 per semester.
Hostel fees increase by ₹4,000 every year. Students must pay fees on time to avoid fines.
Tags: hostel, fee, fees, cost, price, ac, non-ac, payment, fine, 80000, 104000, semester

## HOSTEL-002
Q: What are the room types, allotment, and room change rules?
A: Non-AC (₹80,000/yr) and AC (₹1,04,000/semester) room options are available. Room change is allowed only once in a semester. Allocation of AC seats is based on a 1-year consent during which students cannot leave their AC seat. Moving from a single-seater to a four-seater or three-seater is not permitted. Hostel room booking is managed via the UHostel app.
Tags: room, types, ac, non-ac, allotment, single, double, triple, room change, uhostel, seat

## HOSTEL-003
Q: What items are permitted or prohibited in hostel rooms?
A: Permitted: Hair dryers, hair straighteners, a small bottle of hair oil (mustard oil for hair only; cooking is strictly prohibited), and up to ₹500 cash. Prohibited: All other electrical appliances, medicines without prescription, syringes, jewellery, expensive electronic articles, lethal objects or weapons (immediate expulsion), and alcohol/smoking/intoxicants (suspension/expulsion).
Tags: rules, permitted, prohibited, appliances, cash, weapons, smoking, drinking, drugs, hair dryer, straightener, medicines

## HOSTEL-004
Q: What is the room cleaning schedule and housekeeping rules?
A: Room cleaning comes everyday except Saturday and Sunday. Students must leave their room keys at the hostel reception/keyboard for housekeeping staff to clean the room. Beds must be made before leaving the room, and room layout/bed arrangement must not be altered. Lights, fans, and balcony lights must be switched off when leaving the room.
Tags: housekeeping, cleaning, schedule, key, reception, maintenance, electricity, beds, Saturday, Sunday

## HOSTEL-005
