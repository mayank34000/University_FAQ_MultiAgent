### Room Allotment & Change Policy
- Room allocation and hostel booking are managed digitally via the UHostel app.
- Allocation of AC seats is based on a mandatory 1-year binding consent during which students cannot vacate or downgrade to a Non-AC seat.
- Room changes are permitted only once per semester during designated official transfer windows.
- Mid-semester room swaps or transfers without written approval from the Warden are strictly prohibited.
- Moving from a single-seater to a four-seater or three-seater room is not permitted.
- Hostel fees increase by ₹4,000 every academic year for both AC and Non-AC categories to cover operational and maintenance costs.

### Furniture & Basic Amenities Provided
- Each student is provided with a bed, a study table, a study chair, a wardrobe/cupboard, a ceiling fan, and standard room lighting.
- Soft mattresses, pillows, and bedsheets must be brought by the student.
- Altering the fixed room layout or bed arrangement in hostel rooms is not permitted.

### Permitted & Prohibited Items
- Permitted items: Hair dryers, hair straighteners, a small bottle of hair oil (mustard oil for hair only), and up to ₹500 cash.
- Strictly prohibited electrical appliances: Electric heaters, induction cooktops, electric kettles, irons, hot plates, refrigerators, and immersion rods. Unauthorized appliances will be confiscated.
- Prohibited items & substances: Medicines without a valid doctor's prescription, syringes, expensive jewellery, lethal objects or weapons (immediate expulsion), and alcohol, tobacco, cigarettes, or drugs (suspension/expulsion).
- Cooking food inside hostel rooms is strictly prohibited.

## Category: Housekeeping, Keys & Vacations

### Housekeeping & Room Keys
- Housekeeping cleans rooms every day except Saturday and Sunday.
- Students must drop their room keys at the hostel reception/keyboard before 9:00 AM on weekdays for cleaning staff. Keys are collected upon return.
- Beds must be made before leaving the room, and lights, fans, and balcony lights must be switched off.
- Intentional damage to hostel property or furniture is charged to the responsible student along with a disciplinary fine.
- A lost key must be reported to the Caretaker; replacement key or lock replacement charges apply.

### Vacation Stay & Luggage Storage
- Hostel stay during summer or winter break is allowed only for approved research, internships, or summer courses with written permission from the Chief Warden.
- Centralized luggage store rooms are available in each hostel block during summer break. Luggage must be properly locked and labeled.

## Category: Hostel Rules, Gate Pass & Attendance

### Night Attendance & Silence Hours
- Mandatory physical night attendance takes place between 10:00 PM and 11:00 PM every night.
- Strict silence hours are observed from 12:15 AM to 6:15 AM. Loud music and high-power Bluetooth speakers are prohibited.

### Online Gate Pass Rules
- All gate passes must be applied online via the UHostel app 24 hours in advance. No manual gate passes are issued.
- Gate pass application window on UHostel app: 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM daily.
- Warden approval windows: 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM, 4:00 PM to 5:00 PM, and 9:00 PM to 10:00 PM.
- Sunday / Holiday gate pass deadline: Apply before 12:00 PM noon on the preceding day.
- No gate passes are issued or generated after 7:30 PM under normal circumstances.
- Automatic SMS and app notifications are sent to parents upon application. Emergency passes require direct parent approval.
- All gate pass movement must be scanned and logged at the University Main Gate security kiosk using the student QR code on the UHostel app.

### Visitors, Food Delivery & Celebrations
- Day scholars, outside guests, and parents are strictly NOT permitted inside hostel rooms or hostel premises. Bringing a day scholar inside the hostel invites disciplinary action.
- Outside commercial food delivery (Swiggy, Zomato) is strictly prohibited inside hostel rooms and premises.
- Square One food must be consumed at Square One or the Dining Hall, not in hostel rooms.
- Birthday celebrations, cake cutting, and room parties are strictly prohibited inside hostel rooms and corridors.

## Category: Discipline, Safety & Administration

### Conduct & Anti-Ragging Policy
- Ragging (physical, verbal, or psychological) is illegal under UGC regulations, resulting in police reporting and expulsion.
- Ragging can be reported to the Chief Warden, University Anti-Ragging Committee, or National Anti-Ragging Helpline.
- Physical violence or fighting results in immediate suspension and hostel expulsion.
- Misbehavior with wardens, caretakers, mess staff, or security guards is strictly penalized.

### Security, ID Cards & Administration Contacts
- 24x7 CCTV cameras monitor all hostel entrance gates, corridors, and common spaces.
- Displaying physical University ID card is mandatory across campus. Lost ID cards must be reported to security and Registrar's office for a duplicate.
- 24x7 Campus Security Control Room helpline is available for emergencies.
- Residential Administration Contacts: Warden office hours are 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM on working days. Escalation chain: Director (Residential Dept), Director (Discipline), Director (ORS), VP (Residential Dept).

## Category: Dining & Mess Facilities

### Mess Timings & Food Rules
- Standard Mess Meal Timings: Breakfast: 7:30 AM to 9:30 AM | Lunch: 12:30 PM to 2:30 PM | Evening Snacks: 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM | Dinner: 7:30 PM to 9:30 PM.
- Mess meal timings are extended by 30 minutes during end-semester exams.
- Mess menu is updated every quarter (every 3 months) based on student committee feedback.
- Physical mess cards must display a clear student photograph for verification.
- Mess plates, glasses, and steel cutlery must not be carried into hostel rooms.
- Light sick diet (khichdi, daliya, porridge, bread, milk) can be requested at the mess counter with a dispensary medical slip.
- Visiting parents can purchase single meal guest coupons at the Mess Manager office.
- Mess fee rebate is granted for approved leave of 3 consecutive days or more with prior application.
- RO water purifiers and coolers are installed on every floor of all hostel blocks.
- Hostel night canteens serve snacks, tea, coffee, and hot beverages until 2:00 AM.
- Campus food outlets operate 24x7.

## Category: Sportatorium & Gym Facilities

### Sportatorium Facilities & Pool Table Rates
- Sports courts and grounds are bookable on an hourly basis by scanning the court QR code at the sports desk.
- Equipment Issue Blackout Hours: Sports equipment CANNOT be issued during lunch break (1:00 PM to 2:00 PM) and dinner break (8:30 PM to 9:30 PM).
- Pool Table Fees: 1 Hour (2 Persons ₹80, 4 Persons ₹120, 6 Persons ₹160) | Half Hour (2 Persons ₹50, 4 Persons ₹80, 6 Persons ₹120).
- Non-marking rubber sole sports shoes are compulsory for synthetic indoor badminton courts.
- Outdoor basketball and volleyball courts operate daily from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM under LED floodlights (turned off at 9:00 PM).
- Swimming Pool Hours: 6:00 AM to 9:00 AM and 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM (nylon/lycra swimwear mandatory).
- Indoor recreation lounge includes Chess, Carrom, and Foosball.

### Campus Gym Membership & Timings
- Gym memberships are purchased exclusively via the UCampus App.
- Gym Membership Pricing: 6 Months: ₹8,900 | 1 Year: ₹11,400 | (Note: 3-month gym membership packages are NOT offered).
- Gym Operating Hours: Morning Session (6:00 AM to 9:30 AM) | Evening Session (4:30 PM to 9:00 PM) | Closed on Sundays and national public holidays.
- Certified male and female fitness trainers are present. Day lockers (padlock required) and proper athletic sportswear are mandatory.
