## Common placement-related questions

1. **What is the average package at Chitkara University for engineering roles?**
   Based on the data, prominent tech roles range heavily between 8 LPA and upwards to over 40-50 LPA at top companies like Microsoft and ServiceNow. Mass hiring and other roles typically cluster around 5-8 LPA.
2. **What are the common locations for placement?**
   Bangalore, Hyderabad, Gurugram, Pune, and Mumbai are the most prominent hiring locations for Chitkara University students.
3. **Is an internship mandatory or provided by companies?**
   A vast majority of these opportunities begin as internships (often referred to as Summer Internships or Software Engineer Intern roles) spanning 6 months with stipends ranging from 20,000 INR/month up to 125,000 INR/month.
4. **Does CGPA matter during placements?**
   Yes, multiple top-tier drives mandate high CGPAs (e.g., above 8.5 or top 100 students) and specific 10th/12th board averages (often above 75% or 85%).
5. **What is UCA?**
   UCA refers to the specialized/advanced cohorts or training groups within the university program (e.g., University Coding Academy or similar) which often reflect a notable percentage of placed students.

## What information is unavailable?
- Final conversion rates of intern-to-full-time (FTE) offers are not universally specified.
- The precise number of students participating in each drive versus the number of students selected (selection ratio) is unavailable.
- Specific interview rounds (technical, HR, etc.) and difficulty levels for the majority of the companies are missing.

## Data limitations
- **Incomplete Records:** Some company entries lack definitive placement numbers, specific stipends, or CTC data, particularly for the upcoming/recent 2027 batch drives.
- **Subjective Criteria:** Eligibility criteria like "Diversity Hiring" or "Straightforward" are qualitative and do not guarantee exact numerical cut-offs unless specified.
- **Dynamic Offerings:** The stipends and CTC ranges provided are snapshot figures from specific drives. Companies may adjust these packages or the number of hired students based on their requirements year-over-year.
